What it does, in plain words

It's a single HTML file that runs offline. The contractor picks a trade, measures the work, and the tool prices the actual hours, shows real cost and margin, and prints a one-page proposal with three scope tiers. The built-in discount simulator is the demo that sells it: a 10% discount on a $2,478 job at a 40% margin costs about a quarter of the profit, and the tool shows that number before the contractor says yes.
