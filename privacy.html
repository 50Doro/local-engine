Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.

The tool itself

Quote Engine is a single HTML file that runs entirely in your browser. It makes zero network calls. Your job data, rates and settings live in your browser's localStorage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, tracked or shared by the Software.

What we collect

The only personal data involved is the email address you provide at checkout, which is collected by the payment processor you buy through (not by the Software). We use it to deliver the file and to send the post-purchase emails described in the manual.

Analytics

The sales page may use basic, cookieless analytics to count page views. No personal data is collected by it, and it can be blocked without breaking anything.

Third parties

Payments are processed by the checkout provider you choose (WarriorPlus, JVZoo, Stripe, Gumroad, Payhip or Lemon Squeezy). Their privacy policies govern the checkout. The Software itself has no third-party integrations.

Your rights

You can request deletion of your checkout email at any time by emailing ayoubebay1@gmail.com. Since the Software stores data only on your device, deleting it there removes everything else.
